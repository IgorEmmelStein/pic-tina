## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many simulator hours count toward my Private Pilot certificate?**
FAA regulations allow up to 2.5 hours of AATD simulator time to count toward the Private Pilot certificate.

**Can I use the $60/hour rate for just 5 hours?**
The $60 rate applies to block purchases of 10 or more hours. Smaller blocks are available at the standard hourly rate. Contact PIC for current pricing.

**Is the free checkout required?**
The simulator checkout is normally required for first-time users to ensure safety and efficiency. With the limited-time offer, it is included at no additional cost when you purchase 10+ hours.

**Do I need my own instructor for simulator time?**
You can use PIC’s CFIs for simulator lessons, or if you are already a rated pilot, you may be able to practice independently after checkout (subject to PIC’s policies).

**Can I split my 10-hour block with another student?**
Block rates are generally tied to one student’s account. Contact PIC to discuss shared options.
