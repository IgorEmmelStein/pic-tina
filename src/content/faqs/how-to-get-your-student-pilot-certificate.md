faqs:
  - question: "What is a student pilot certificate?"
    answer: "A student pilot certificate is the FAA-issued credential that allows a beginner to start flight training. Often referred to as a pilot permit, it is the essential first formal step for anyone looking to learn to fly and eventually earn their <a href='/flight-training/private-pilot/' class='underline font-bold !text-accent-500 hover:text-primary-800'>Private Pilot License</a>. Learn more about this milestone in our <a href='/blog/private-pilot-license-requirements-what-to-know-before-you-start/' class='underline font-bold !text-accent-500 hover:text-primary-800'>Student Pilot Certificate guide</a>."
  
  - question: "How do I apply for a student pilot certificate?"
    answer: "The process begins with completing the FAA application and submitting the required personal information. Once accepted, you will have the documentation needed to start logging flight time. Your flight school will help you understand the specific steps and documents required. Check out our full article on <a href='/blog/how-to-get-your-student-pilot-certificate-and-start-your-private-pilot-license-journey/' class='underline font-bold !text-accent-500 hover:text-primary-800'>how the application process works</a>."
  
  - question: "Do I need a medical certificate to start flying?"
    answer: "Yes, before you can fly under training privileges, you will typically need to pass an examination with an FAA-authorized medical examiner. This ensures the training environment is safe and appropriate for your experience level. We explain why this step matters in our <a href='/blog/how-to-get-your-student-pilot-certificate-and-start-your-private-pilot-license-journey/' class='underline font-bold !text-accent-500 hover:text-primary-800'>guide to starting your Private Pilot journey</a>."
  
  - question: "What happens after I get my student pilot certificate?"
    answer: "Once you have your certificate, you can begin building actual flight experience! This includes ground instruction, <a href='/about/our-fleet/simulator-redbird-fmx-full-motion-aatd/' class='underline font-bold !text-accent-500 hover:text-primary-800'>simulator practice</a>, and hands-on flight lessons with a certified instructor. This structured experience lays the foundation for you to meet the required milestones for your <a href='/flight-training/private-pilot/' class='underline font-bold !text-accent-500 hover:text-primary-800'>Private Pilot License</a>."
  
  - question: "Why should I start my pilot training with PIC?"
    answer: "At Pilot Instructional Center, we guide you through every step of the process, from your <a href='/discovery-flight/' class='underline font-bold !text-accent-500 hover:text-primary-800'>Discovery Flight</a> and FAA paperwork to your first solo flight. We provide clear instruction, structured lessons, and a supportive environment to make your path from student pilot to private pilot manageable and practical."
