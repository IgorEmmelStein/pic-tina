All images are in standard format
Search engines support common image formats like jpeg, jpg, png, gif, svg, ico and webp. Avoid using non-standard image formats.

-
- All images are in modern format
  AVIF and WebP are image formats that have superior compression and quality characteristics compared to their older JPEG and PNG counterparts. Encoding your images in these formats means that they will load faster and consume less cellular data.
-
- All images served through HTTPS
  All images on an HTTPS page should also be served through HTTPS.
