# Aviation Knowledge Checklist

Populate the files under `flight-school-knowledge/` before relying on this skill for production fact-checking.

## Required Knowledge Areas

- FAA certificate and rating pathway
- Part 61 vs Part 141 training differences
- Private, instrument, commercial, CFI, CFII, MEI, multi-engine terms
- Checkrides and Designated Pilot Examiners
- FAA medical certificate basics
- Airline career path and hour-building claims
- Aircraft and training environment terminology
- Training cost and financing claim risks
- Safety, maintenance, and operational claim risks
- Official FAA and aviation source register

## Use Rule

If a reference file is not populated with enough evidence for a claim, classify the claim as `needs source`.
