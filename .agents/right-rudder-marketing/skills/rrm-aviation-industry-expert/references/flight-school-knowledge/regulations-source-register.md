# Regulations Source Register

Use this register before classifying FAA, medical, checkride, training-requirement, or career-path claims as `verified`.

Reviewed on: 2026-04-29

## Source Priority For Aviation Claims

- FAA and eCFR sources are the rule source for certificates, ratings, training requirements, medical certificates, checkrides, DPEs, maintenance, and operating rules.
- eCFR pages are authoritative but unofficial online versions of the CFR. Use them for current rule text, then cite the relevant CFR section in fact-check notes.
- AOPA, EAA, BLS, and similar sources may support explanatory or industry-context claims, but do not override FAA/eCFR requirements.
- Client-specific claims, including fleet, pricing, housing, staff, school approvals, DPE access, financing, local airport details, and program inclusions, require current client documentation.

## High-Value CFR Sections To Check

- Student pilots: 14 CFR 61.83, 61.85, 61.87, 61.89, 61.93.
- Medical certificate requirement and duration: 14 CFR 61.23; BasicMed conditions in 61.113(i) and Part 68.
- Additional aircraft ratings and endorsements: 14 CFR 61.31 and 61.63.
- Instrument rating: 14 CFR 61.65.
- Practical tests: 14 CFR 61.39, 61.43, 61.45, 61.47, 61.49.
- Designated pilot examiners: 14 CFR 183.23.
- Private pilot: 14 CFR 61.103, 61.105, 61.107, 61.109, 61.113.
- Commercial pilot: 14 CFR 61.123, 61.125, 61.127, 61.129, 61.133.
- Airline transport pilot and restricted ATP: 14 CFR 61.153, 61.156, 61.157, 61.159, 61.160, 61.167.
- Flight instructor: 14 CFR 61.183, 61.185, 61.187, 61.189, 61.191, 61.193, 61.195, 61.197.
- Part 141 courses: Part 141 appendices B, C, D, F, G, I, M.
- General operating rules: 14 CFR 91.3, 91.7, 91.103, 91.126, 91.127, 91.129, 91.130, 91.131, 91.155, 91.167, 91.173.
- Maintenance and inspection: 14 CFR 91.403, 91.405, 91.407, 91.409, 91.417; Part 43.
