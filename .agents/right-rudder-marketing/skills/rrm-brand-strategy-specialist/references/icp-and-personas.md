## Flight School Persona Patterns

- Career-switcher comparing training timelines and financing
- High school or college-age student evaluating career paths
- Parent or partner helping assess risk, cost, housing, and outcomes
- Licensed pilot adding a rating or instructor certificate
- Out-of-state student evaluating relocation and schedule reliability

Use only patterns supported by the client's content. If a persona is strategically likely but unsupported, label it as an inference.
