# Proof And Claims Extraction

The strategist must separate usable proof from attractive but unsupported marketing.

## Safe Claim Types

- Client-owned facts visible in current repo content
- Program names and offer details from current pages
- Testimonials or reviews with source files
- Staff, fleet, facility, or location facts from current pages
- SEO/query data from supplied exports

## Risky Claim Types

- Best, fastest, safest, cheapest, guaranteed
- FAA, legal, medical, or career outcome claims without official support
- Salary, job placement, hiring demand, pass rate, or safety claims
- Competitor claims without evidence
- Time-sensitive pricing or regulation claims

## Extraction Format

```markdown
- Claim:
- Source path:
- Type: safe | risky | needs client confirmation | needs aviation fact-check
- Why it matters:
- Suggested use:
```
