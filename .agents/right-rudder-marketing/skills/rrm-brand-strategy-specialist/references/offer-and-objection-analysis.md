# Offer And Objection Analysis

Use this reference to convert site content into useful offer and objection strategy.

## Offer Extraction

For each offer, capture:
- Name
- Buyer
- Outcome
- Timeline, price, inclusions, or constraints if sourced
- What happens next
- CTA path
- Proof or trust signal

## Objection Categories

- Cost and financing
- Timeline and schedule reliability
- Safety and maintenance
- Training quality
- Instructor consistency
- Checkride availability
- Housing or relocation
- Career uncertainty
- Eligibility and medical requirements

## Output Rule

Answer objections only with sourced evidence. If the site does not support an answer, write the best question to ask the client.
